# DRIFT Privacy Policy

DRIFT does not collect personal information.

The app creates techno patterns and plays sound on your device. Settings such as tempo, intensity, key, and style stay inside the app. DRIFT does not require an account, does not send user data to a server, and does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this repository.
