# DRIFT Privacy Policy

DRIFT does not collect personal information.

The app creates techno patterns and plays sound on your device. Settings such as tempo, intensity, key, and style stay inside the app. DRIFT does not require an account and does not send generated patterns to a server.

DRIFT uses Google AdMob to show ads. Google may receive data needed to serve and measure ads, such as device identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics, and approximate location depending on device settings. Google handles this data under its own privacy policies.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this repository.
